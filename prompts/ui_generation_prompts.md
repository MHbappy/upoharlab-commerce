## Prompt 1: Global Design System & Layout (Base Prompt)
*Paste this first to establish the global styling, Header, and Footer before generating specific pages.*

**Prompt:**
> "I want to build a modern, premium corporate website for an engineering and renewable energy company named 'Suffix Engineering Limited'. Create a base HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS template. 
> 
> **Design System:**
> - **Primary Color:** Vibrant Green (`#00A651`) - use for buttons, icons, highlight text, and active states.
> - **Secondary Color:** Deep Navy Blue (`#0B1B36`) - use for the footer background, hero overlays, and primary headings.
> - **Backgrounds:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Light Gray (`#F5F7FA`) for alternating sections.
> - **Typography:** Use Google Fonts 'Inter' or 'Outfit'. Headings should be bold and modern; body text should be clean and readable (size 16px, color `#4A5568`).
> - **UI Style:** Cards should have a white background, rounded corners (8px), a subtle box-shadow on hover, and light gray borders. Use glassmorphism or smooth gradients for hero overlays.
> 
> **Components to generate in this step:**
> 1. **Header (Navbar):** Fixed at the top, white background. Logo on the far left. Centered navigation links (Home, About Us, Our Business, Products, Projects, Services, Sustainability, Careers, Contact) with a green underline effect on hover/active. On the far right, a search icon and a solid green 'Get a Quote ->' button.
> 2. **Footer:** Deep Navy background. Divided into columns: Logo with a short description, 'Quick Links', 'Our Solutions', 'Connect With Us' (Address, Phone, Email with icons), and 'Follow Us' (Social media icons). A bottom border separating the copyright text and a 'back to top' arrow button.
> 
> Please output the `index.css` containing these global variables and the HTML shell with the Header and Footer."
