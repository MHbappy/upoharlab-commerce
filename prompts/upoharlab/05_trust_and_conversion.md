# Phase 5: clarity and usability pass

Follow `00_project_rules.md`. This phase improves the existing screens; it adds no database tables or marketing systems.

## Make the customer journey easy to understand

- Home quickly answers what is sold and how to order. Real product cards are visible without a long promotional introduction.
- A product page clearly answers: what do I get, what can be engraved, what does it cost including delivery, when does making start and what happens after ordering?
- Keep price, delivery cost and payment timing visible. Put the form near essential product information; move optional care/FAQ content below it.
- Use simple Bangla labels and short explanations. Replace mixed labels such as “approve ও advance” with “সম্মতি ও অগ্রিম”.
- Show one main action per section. The WhatsApp link is available as help without competing with every order button.
- Error messages explain how to fix the field, preserve the customer's work and move focus usefully. Success clearly states that the order was saved.

## Trust through useful facts

Use real photos, material/size, actual making time, business contact details and the design-before-payment explanation. Name plates show the indoor-only limitation.

Only publish owner-confirmed cancellation, revision and damage terms. Do not add a free-remake promise just because it sounds reassuring. Hide unavailable optional photos/story content cleanly.

No reviews system, ratings, sales counts, scarcity badges, countdowns, offers or capacity meter. No placeholder sections inviting a later agent to build them.

## Responsive and accessibility check

Check at 320px, 390px, tablet width and about 1280px. Product cards and photo/detail layouts should use the available width; reading text and forms should stay comfortable.

Check Bangla conjuncts, text contrast, visible keyboard focus, persistent labels, error announcements, touch targets and image alt text. The form and summary must work with keyboard navigation, mobile zoom and JavaScript disabled. No sticky element should obstruct the form.

## Done when

Walk through home → product → order → confirmation and fix the observed friction. Report the screens/widths checked and remaining issues. Do not add screenshot tests or a new testing framework just for this styling/copy pass.
